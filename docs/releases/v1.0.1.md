A fix release of the unofficial native PC port of 007: NightFire (original Xbox).

## The build stopped at "Driving.xbe": fixed

Version 1.0.0's launcher took only `default.xbe` from your disc, so every build stopped while preparing, with
`file SHA1 failed to read file ... assets/Driving.xbe` in the launcher's log, on every disc. NightFire is two
programs (the driving missions are `Driving.xbe`), and the launcher now takes both from your disc, builds both, and
installs both (`Nightfire.exe` and `NightfireDriving.exe`) into `NightFire\play`.

## Updating

Open **NightFireLauncher.exe** (the 1.0.0 one you have): it offers this release at the top. Press **Update the
launcher**, and when it has restarted press **Build**. Or download **NightFireLauncher.exe** below and put it in place
of the old one. Your disc choice, saves and settings stay as they are.

No game files are included. You need your own copy of the game (the European PAL or the USA release). See the
[v1.0.0 notes](https://github.com/hk21x/nightfire-recomp/releases/tag/v1.0.0) for everything in the port.

Problems: the game's log is in `NightFire\play\logs`, the launcher's in `NightFire\launcher.log`.
