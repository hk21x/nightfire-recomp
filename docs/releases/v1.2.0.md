This release adds stick settings for your controller, a button that unlocks everything the game's secret codes give,
better Deep Descent controls on the keyboard, and fills 16:10 screens.

## Controller stick settings

**F1 → Controls → Controller sticks** has a dead zone and a sensitivity for each stick (issue #10).

- **Dead zone** (0 to 50%): small movements near the centre are ignored, so a stick that drifts stops nudging your
  aim. The rest of the stick's travel still reaches full speed.
- **Sensitivity** (25 to 300%): how fast the stick turns and moves Bond. At 50%, full tilt turns at about half the
  game's speed. The game's own dead zone is taken into account, so low settings still move.

They're also in `settings.toml` as `left_stick_deadzone`, `right_stick_deadzone`, `left_stick_sensitivity` and
`right_stick_sensitivity`. The defaults leave the sticks as the game reads them.

## Unlock All Codes

**PC Settings → Unlock All Codes** (in the main menu) enters all 52 of the game's secret codes at once and keeps
everything they unlock (issue #9). Pressing it again leaves your unlocks as they are.

## Deep Descent on the keyboard

In the Deep Descent submarine level, the **up arrow** (or Left Shift) now rises and the **down arrow** (or Left Ctrl)
dives, as you'd expect on a keyboard (issue #12). A controller still dives with the stick pushed up, as on the Xbox.

## Also in this release

- 16:10 screens (and others between 4:3 and 16:9, like the Steam Deck's) are filled instead of letterboxed.
- **Alt+Enter** switches between a window and full screen.
- Smoother frame rates in busy scenes: more of the drawing work runs on its own thread.
- Crash reports hold more of what's needed to fix a crash, and the last five runs' logs are kept.

## Updating

Open **NightFireLauncher.exe**. It offers this release at the top: press **Update the launcher** if it asks, then
**Update**. Your disc choice, saves and settings stay as they are.

New players: download **NightFireLauncher.exe** below, choose your disc and press **Get the game ready**.

## What's in this release

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `NightFireLauncher.exe` | **The only file to download by hand.** It fetches the rest itself. |
| `NightFire-package.zip` | The runtime and the compiler, for both discs, and no game code. The launcher fetches it. |
| `NightFire-package.zip.json` | The size and SHA-256 the launcher checks the download against. |

No game files are included. You need your own copy of the game (the European PAL or the USA release). See the
[v1.0.0 notes](https://github.com/hk21x/nightfire-recomp/releases/tag/v1.0.0) for everything in the port.

Help and reports: the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/PQjkKsdtc). Problems: the game's log is in
`NightFire\play\logs`, the launcher's in `NightFire\launcher.log`.
