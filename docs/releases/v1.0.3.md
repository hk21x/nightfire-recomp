An update of the unofficial native PC port of 007: NightFire (original Xbox).

## The driving missions at 60 FPS on the European disc

On the European (PAL) disc, the driving missions ran at 50 frames a second, as they did on a PAL Xbox: the game times
its driving half by the TV standard it finds. They now run at 60, as the USA disc always did, with the game's speed
and timers unchanged. The European disc's own movies and speech are still used. The on-foot missions were already at
60.

## A new settings window, and more graphics options

- The settings window (F1) is redone: a list of sections, full-width rows and a help line for each, and it can be
  used with a controller.
- New graphics options, all off by default: **SMAA**, a **sharp scaling** filter, **alpha edges with MSAA** (smoother
  fences and foliage), **low latency**, and **Enhanced world textures** (sharper world textures; it can cause short
  hitches while new textures load).
- **F12** saves a screenshot of the game as a PNG in a `screenshots` folder beside the game.
- Faster drawing in busy scenes.

## The launcher

The launcher has a new look to match, and its log now times each step of a build.

## Updating

Open **NightFireLauncher.exe**: it should offer this release at the top. Press **Update the launcher**, and when it has
restarted press **Build**. If no offer appears, download **NightFireLauncher.exe** below and put it in place of the old
one. Your disc choice, saves and settings stay as they are.

No game files are included. You need your own copy of the game (the European PAL or the USA release). See the
[v1.0.0 notes](https://github.com/hk21x/nightfire-recomp/releases/tag/v1.0.0) for everything in the port.

Help and reports: the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/PQjkKsdtc). Problems: the game's log is in
`NightFire\play\logs`, the launcher's in `NightFire\launcher.log`.
