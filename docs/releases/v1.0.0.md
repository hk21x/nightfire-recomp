The first release of the unofficial native PC port of 007: NightFire (original Xbox): the game's own code
statically recompiled into Windows programs, not an emulator.

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game (the European PAL release): an `.iso` of your
disc, or a folder with its files. The game is built on your PC from your disc.

## Getting started

1. Download **NightFireLauncher.exe** below and put it in a folder of its own.
2. Run it. Windows may warn about an unrecognised app: **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Choose your disc image (or folder), accept Microsoft's license for its C++ libraries, press **Build**. The first
   build takes 30 to 90 minutes.
4. Press **Play**. **PC Settings** is in the game's main menu; **F1** opens the full settings window at any time.

## What's in it

- **On foot and driving**: the on-foot missions and the driving missions (Paris Prelude, Alpine Escape, Enemies
  Vanquished, Deep Descent, Island Infiltration), with the switch between the two halves of the game done for you.
- **Widescreen and ultrawide**, on foot and in the driving missions: the view widens to the screen, the HUD and menus
  keep their 16:9 shape in the centre. A **field of view** setting, and 60 FPS.
- **Keyboard and mouse like a PC shooter**: direct mouse aiming with no auto-aim, a slower mouse when zoomed, the
  mouse wheel for weapons, a guided missile that steers with the mouse, the mounted guns aimed with the mouse, and a
  separate key layout for driving. Every key can be rebound.
- **Your keys on screen**: the game's button pictures in tutorials, menus and controls pages show the keys and mouse
  buttons while you play on the keyboard, and switch back for a controller.
- **Clean text**: the game's font redrawn at high resolution (Sour Gummy Black), with sharp HUD pictures.
- **PC Settings in the game's own menus**: field of view, mouse sensitivity and invert, display mode, resolution,
  vsync, frame cap and more.
- **Controllers**: Xbox, PlayStation and Switch, with hot plugging and rumble.
- **Achievements**: 19, with pop-ups and a list in the settings window.
- **Direct3D 12 or Direct3D 11**: pick the renderer in the settings window (it applies at the next start).
- **Quality of life**: Quit game in the settings window, skip the intro logos, a shader cache so later sessions run
  without hitching.

## Known limits

- Only the European (PAL) release is supported; other releases have not been checked.
- Not every mission has been played through to the end on PC yet; please report anything that goes wrong.
- Achievements are awarded when a mission is completed; loading a save where missions are already done awards
  nothing until the next completion.

Saves from a build of your own: copy its `hdd` folder into `NightFire\play`, so the save is at
`NightFire\play\hdd\E\UDATA\45410026\...`.

Problems: the game's log is in `NightFire\play\logs`, the launcher's in `NightFire\launcher.log`.
